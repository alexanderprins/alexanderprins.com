The number is a score out of 100

From my rubric. Each of six things is rated 1 to 5, then weighted: Fit ×5, Odds ×4, Comp ×3, Company ×3, Warm path ×3, Energy ×2.

What the score means

75 and up: apply first, full tailored page. 55 to 74: apply, reuse a page. Under 55 goes to Skipped.

Marks stay in this browser
